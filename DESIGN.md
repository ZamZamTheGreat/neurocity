# NeuroCity interface system

NeuroCity should feel like dependable Namibian commerce infrastructure: premium enough for banks and mall operators, approachable enough for a first-time local merchant, and quick enough for everyday shopping.

## Product identities

- NeuroCity platform: warm black, parchment and restrained gold. Used for the network, account centre, administration and digital-mall management.
- NeuroCity Marketplace: deep navy-black, white and emerald. Used for shopping, catalogue discovery, storefronts and Selma.
- White-labelled malls: inherit each mall's controlled brand tokens while preserving NeuroCity interaction patterns.

## Foundations

- Use the system sans-serif stack. Headlines are tightly tracked; body copy remains spacious and readable.
- Minimum interactive height is 44 pixels. Mobile form controls use 16-pixel text to prevent browser zoom.
- Primary actions are filled, secondary actions outlined, and destructive actions visually separated and explicitly confirmed.
- Gold indicates platform guidance and administrative priority. Emerald indicates commerce, availability and success. Red is reserved for destructive or failed states.
- Use soft warm surfaces, thin borders and restrained shadows. Avoid nested cards when grouping and spacing are sufficient.
- Dashboard numbers use tabular figures. Statuses always combine words with colour; colour is never the sole indicator.

## Interaction rules

- Every asynchronous action must show a busy state, prevent duplicate submission and end with a clear success or recovery message.
- Navigation must work with keyboard, touch and standard left click.
- Empty states explain what is missing and offer the next useful action.
- Errors explain what happened in plain language and how to recover.
- Preserve entered form data when validation fails.
- Respect reduced-motion and increased-contrast preferences.

## Responsive rules

- Desktop dashboards use side navigation and a focused working canvas.
- Mobile dashboards use a drawer, one primary task per viewport and full-width actions.
- Dense tables become readable cards or horizontal regions rather than compressed columns.
- The mobile dock remains clear of device safe areas and never covers the last page action.

## Voice

Use calm, direct language. Prefer “Upload bank confirmation” over “Continue,” “No orders yet” over “No data,” and “Try again” with a recovery explanation over technical errors.
